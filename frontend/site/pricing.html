Questions

What is a seat?

A seat is one person with an account in your hosted workspace. Add or remove seats at any time.

Is self-hosting really free?

Yes. Kanforge is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license, so you can run every app on your own server at no cost. You're responsible for running, updating and backing it up.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Move between Standard and Plus whenever you like. Your boards, notes and documents stay where they are.

What about Files and Mail?

They're coming soon. We'll announce pricing for them when they're ready.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Export notes as Markdown, and download documents as Word or Excel files.
