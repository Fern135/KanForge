Your boards, notes and documents, on your own server.

Kanforge is an open-source suite of apps behind one sign-in: kanban boards, notes, and an office suite with docs and sheets. Host it yourself for free, or let us run it for you.

Get started View on GitHub

One sign-in, every app

Switch between apps from the home screen or the navbar. Admins can turn each app on or off for everyone.

Boards

Kanban boards with lists, cards, labels, due dates, checklists and comments. Share a board by inviting people by email.

Notes

Rich-text notes with nested folders, tags, search and pinning. Import Markdown or an Obsidian vault, and export everything as a .zip.

Office

Docs and Sheets that work like Word and Excel. Open and save .docx, .xlsx and CSV files, with about 140 spreadsheet functions.

Files

Mail

Email in the same place as the rest of your work.

Security first, and yours to run

Argon2id passwords, rotating refresh tokens, CSRF protection, rate limits, a strict Content Security Policy, and non-root read-only containers. The self-hosted version makes no outside requests: no analytics, no trackers, no phoning home.
